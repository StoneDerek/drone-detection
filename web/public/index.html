Both plots use a shared linear Hz display; values from the model's original linear or mel bins are projected onto that axis without adding spectral detail. Warm overlay colours mark positive attribution to the selected logit. Maps are normalized independently; brightness is relative within a map. A zero map is reported rather than filled with invented detail. Grad-CAM is computed from gradients of the trained CNN, at the selected layer's native resolution; interpolation adds no explanatory detail. Attribution can reveal shortcuts but does not prove a useful or causal acoustic cue.
